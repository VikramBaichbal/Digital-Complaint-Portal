About the Gram Panchayat Portal

The Digital Complaint Portal for Gram Panchayat is a centralized e-governance initiative designed to modernize public service delivery and streamline citizen-administration communication at the village level. By digitizing the traditional paper-based grievance process, the platform ensures that civic concerns are documented, routed, and resolved with full accountability.

Residents can submit detailed reports regarding essential village infrastructure—including drinking water supply, road maintenance, street lighting, drainage, sanitation, and public utilities—from anywhere at any time, complete with photographic evidence and exact location markers.

Key Objectives

  • Accessible Citizen Services: Eliminate the need for repeated physical visits to Panchayat offices by enabling 24/7 online grievance submission.
  • End-to-End Transparency: Provide every registered complaint with a unique reference ID (e.g., GP-2026-XXXX) for real-time status tracking.
  • Evidence-Based Reporting: Support geo-tagged location details and photographic uploads to help field staff diagnose and prioritize issues accurately.
  • Accountable Administration: Maintain a permanent, time-stamped audit trail of status transitions, staff assignments, and official resolution remarks.
  • Data-Driven Governance: Equip Panchayat officials with centralized analytics to identify recurring village infrastructure bottlenecks and allocate resources effectively.
  • Secure Data Privacy: Enforce strict role-based access controls so citizen personal records remain protected while administrative workflows stay organized.

How the Redressal Workflow Operates

1. Citizen Submission

Residents register their issue by category, specify the landmark, attach supporting photos, and receive an instant tracking ID.

2. Official Review & Dispatch

Panchayat administrators verify the report, update its status to Under Review or In Progress, and assign field personnel.

3. Resolution & Audit Log

Once resolved on the ground, officials close the ticket with detailed remarks visible directly on the citizen's tracking timeline.
