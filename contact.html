Gram Panchayat Support Desk

For assistance with online grievance registration, tracking an existing reference ID, or reporting urgent civic infrastructure emergencies, please contact the Gram Panchayat administrative office during working hours.

Quick Citizen Guidelines

  • Keep your Complaint Reference ID (e.g., GP-2026-XXXX) ready when inquiring about status updates.
  • Attach clear photographs and accurate street or ward landmarks when submitting a grievance to speed up field verification.
  • For life-threatening electrical hazards or major water pipeline bursts, call the Toll-Free Helpdesk directly for immediate dispatch.
